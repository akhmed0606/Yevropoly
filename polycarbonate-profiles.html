System Overview

Profiles make the sheet order easier to install and easier to quote.

Multiwall sheet performance depends not only on the sheet itself, but also on edge closure, joining method, fasteners, sealing details, thermal expansion allowance, and drainage.

Yevro can quote matching profile systems alongside multiwall sheet orders so buyers can source more of the project package from one supply channel.

  • H-profiles for joining two multiwall sheets in the field.
  • U-profiles for closing and finishing open sheet edges.
  • Snap cap systems for cleaner glazing lines and stronger system presentation.
  • Accessories including washers, sealing tape, and breather tape where required.
