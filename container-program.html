What it is

A more practical way to plan larger polycarbonate orders.

The Container Program is designed for buyers who need more than small retail quantities and want a clearer path for sourcing multiwall and solid polycarbonate sheets for resale, fabrication, greenhouse construction, roofing, or commercial projects.

Instead of treating every larger order as a one-off freight problem, Yevro reviews product requirements, timing, and delivery location to determine whether a container-based supply option is suitable.

  • Planned supply: align larger sheet orders with realistic production, shipment, and delivery windows.
  • Regional coordination: evaluate compatible project and wholesale orders by region and timing.
  • Better landed-cost planning: reduce avoidable freight inefficiencies where container supply makes sense.
  • Formal quoting: receive a clear supply option based on product specification, quantity, destination, and timing.

Who the Program Is For

The Container Program is built for professional buyers planning meaningful sheet volume, not small one-off retail orders.

Project Buyers

Companies ordering sheet material for defined commercial, industrial, agricultural, or institutional projects.

Repeat Purchasing

Buyers who need a more predictable path for larger polycarbonate orders across the year.

How It Works

A simple high-level process. Specific supply options are confirmed by quote.

Submit project details

Send the product type, thickness, color, sheet size, estimated quantity, destination, and preferred timing.

We review fit

Yevro reviews the specifications, quantity, location, timing, and whether a container-based supply option is appropriate.

Receive a quote

We provide a formal supply option with product details, lead time, commercial terms, and delivery assumptions.

Approved orders are planned

Qualified orders are scheduled through the appropriate container supply route based on product, region, and timing.

Note: Container Program availability depends on product specification, volume, production timing, destination, and compatible order windows. Yevro confirms the best available supply option during quoting.
