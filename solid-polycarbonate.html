Product Overview

Clear, strong, and fabrication-friendly.

Yevro Solid Polycarbonate Sheets are a premium alternative to glass and acrylic when the project needs high impact resistance, optical clarity, cold bending, and reliable UV protection.

Solid polycarbonate is commonly used where breakage risk, public exposure, industrial safety, or long-term outdoor durability matters.

  • UV protection on both sides for easier orientation and long-term exterior use.
  • 15-year UV warranty on Yevro solid polycarbonate sheets.
  • High impact resistance for glazing, guards, shields, and demanding applications.
  • Clear and tinted options for fabrication, signage, commercial glazing, and safety barriers.

Key Benefits

  • Virtually unbreakable compared with glass
  • High optical clarity in clear sheets
  • UV protection on both sides
  • Cold-bendable for curved glazing applications
  • Lighter and easier to handle than glass

Common Applications

  • Safety glazing and protective barriers
  • Machine guards and industrial shields
  • Signage, display, and fabrication projects
  • Bus shelters, skylights, and exterior glazing
  • Security panels and high-impact transparent surfaces

A stronger sheet for projects where acrylic or glass may not be enough.

Solid polycarbonate is often chosen when the buyer wants clarity but cannot accept easy cracking or breakage. It can be cut, drilled, formed, and fabricated with the right tooling and installation practices.
