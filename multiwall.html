Product Overview

Built for light, insulation, and impact resistance.

Yevro Multiwall Polycarbonate Sheets are designed for professional projects where buyers need a lightweight glazing material that can transmit light, improve insulation, and handle demanding outdoor conditions better than glass.

Our Canadian program focuses on practical wholesale supply: clear product options, project-based quoting, matching profiles, and freight planning for pallet and container orders.

  • Manufactured from 100% virgin polycarbonate resin no recycled or mixed resin content.
  • H-wall and X-wall options for different insulation, rigidity, and application needs.
  • UV protection by co-extrusion for long-term outdoor exposure.
  • Project-ready logistics with pallet and container supply options across Canada.

Key Benefits

  • Lightweight alternative to glass
  • Thermal insulation from multiwall air chambers
  • High impact resistance for hail-prone and exposed areas
  • Good light transmission for greenhouse and daylighting uses
  • Available in clear, white/opal, grey/bronze tint, green, blue, and custom colours by volume

Common Applications

  • Commercial and agricultural greenhouses
  • Canopies, covered walkways, and carports
  • Skylights, roof glazing, and daylight panels
  • Industrial wall systems and partitions
  • Architectural projects requiring lightweight translucent glazing

H-wall and X-wall structures, quoted around the project.

Use 8mm and 10mm twin-wall sheets for many greenhouse, canopy, and roofing applications. Step up to 16mm or 20mm X-wall where stronger insulation and rigidity are needed. Final thickness depends on span, support spacing, local loads, installation system, and code requirements.

Why Buyers Choose Yevro Multiwall

100% Virgin Resin

Every sheet is extruded exclusively from virgin polycarbonate resin for maximum impact strength, UV stability, and long-term durability.

Co-Extruded UV

UV protection is built into the sheet surface during production, not treated as an afterthought.

Thermal Efficiency

Multiwall chambers help improve insulation compared with single-layer glazing.

Impact Resistance

Designed for exposed outdoor applications where durability matters.

Custom Lengths
