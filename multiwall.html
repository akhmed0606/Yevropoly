Multiwall Polycarbonate Sheets

Polycarbonate multiwall sheets with co-extruded UV protection, available in thickness from 4mm to 20mm. High thermal insulation for professional builds.

Exceptional Thermal Insulation

Engineered for maximum thermal efficiency, Yevro's multiwall polycarbonate sheets utilize an advanced multi-chamber design that drastically reduces heat loss. By trapping air within the flutes, these panels create a powerful insulating barrier that lowers heating costs for Canadian greenhouses and commercial buildings, all without sacrificing natural light transmission.

Manufactured in Europe using 100% Covestro Makrolon® resin, all sheets come with a standard co-extruded UV protective layer on the external surface. This ensures long-term resistance to weathering and yellowing in the Canadian climate.

Benefits

  • 10-Year UV Warranty
  • High thermal insulation
  • Excellent light transmission
  • Lightweight & Durable

Applications
